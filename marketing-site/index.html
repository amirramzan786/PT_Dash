01 / THE PROBLEM

Fitness became fragmented.

One app for workouts. Another for food. Another for recovery. Another for progress. More data did not always create better decisions — it created more places to look.

02 / THE IDEA

What if everything that matters lived in one disciplined system?

Steel was built around a simpler loop: know the work, fuel it, recover from it, and prove that it is moving you forward. The goal is not to gamify your attention. It is to help you execute.

03 / THE FOUNDER

Built for people who want progress without turning fitness into another full-time job.

Project Steel is an independent UK-built product shaped around discipline, consistency, resilience and measurable progress. Early members are not just testing features — they are helping decide what Steel becomes.
