# Project Steel — UK trademark & domain research snapshot

**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Status:** Preliminary brand-clearance research for Project Steel. This is not a substitute for advice from a UK trade mark attorney.

## Current brand decision

Use **PROJECT STEEL** as the primary protectable brand name rather than attempting to register **STEEL** on its own.

Reasoning:
- `STEEL` is a common dictionary word and is commercially crowded.
- `PROJECT STEEL` is more distinctive and gives a stronger filing position.
- The current preliminary search has not surfaced an obvious exact `PROJECT STEEL` fitness/software mark that clearly blocks use, but the search is not exhaustive and similar-mark risk remains.

## Main conflict identified

A material similar mark identified in preliminary research is:

**STEEL PERFORMANCE SOLUTIONS**  
Application number: `UK00801339667`  
Type: word mark  
Reported status: registered  
Classes: 35 and 41  
Relevant Class 41 coverage includes sports performance services, sports performance coaching, sports/business training and related education/training services.

Why it matters:
- It shares the distinctive word `STEEL`.
- It overlaps with fitness/performance/training activity in Class 41.
- This does **not** automatically prevent `PROJECT STEEL`, but it is the clearest opposition/confusion risk identified so far.

Research source used for this indexed record:
- https://www.trademarkelite.com/uk/trademark/trademark-detail/UK00801339667/STEEL-PERFORMANCE-SOLUTIONS

Before filing, verify this mark directly against the current UKIPO register, including its renewal/status position.

## Preliminary risk view

**Overall: manageable / moderate.**

Working assessment:

This is not a legal clearance opinion. UKIPO recommends seeking professional advice if a same/similar mark is known before applying.

## Recommended filing strategy

### Mark

File the **word mark `PROJECT STEEL` first**.

Do not prioritise a logo/device mark yet because the Spartan visual identity may continue to evolve. A word mark is more valuable at this stage because it protects the wording independent of logo changes.

### Target classes

Likely relevant classes:

**Class 9 — downloadable software/apps**  
Draft direction: downloadable mobile applications and software for recording, managing, analysing and tracking workouts, exercise, fitness, nutrition, recovery and physical-progress data.

**Class 41 — fitness/training information**  
Draft direction: provision of fitness and exercise information; provision of online exercise programmes and training information.

Avoid unnecessarily broad wording such as gym facilities, one-to-one personal training, sports-performance consultancy or coaching unless Project Steel genuinely intends to provide those services. Keeping Class 41 wording tighter may reduce overlap with STEEL PERFORMANCE SOLUTIONS.

**Class 42 — SaaS / non-downloadable software**  
Draft direction: SaaS and non-downloadable software for managing, analysing and tracking workout, fitness, nutrition, recovery and progress data.

UKIPO guidance says not to claim goods/services that are not genuinely intended for use within the next five years, and terms cannot simply be expanded after filing.

Source:
- https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark/before-you-apply

## Recommended application route

Use **UKIPO Right Start** rather than filing a standard application blindly.

Reason:
- A similar live mark has already been identified.
- Right Start gives an initial examination before committing the second half of the fee.

### Current 2026 fees

For 3 classes:

**Standard online application**
- First class: £205
- Two additional classes: £60 each
- **Total: £325**

**Right Start**
- Initial stage: £125 + £30 + £30 = **£185**
- If proceeding: another £185
- **Total if completed: £370**

So Right Start costs **£45 more overall** than standard for three classes, but provides the useful early examination checkpoint.

Sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark/start-your-application
- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/trade-mark-forms-and-fees/trade-mark-forms-and-fees

## Renewal

A UK trade mark must be renewed every **10 years**.

Current 2026 online renewal fees:
- First class: £245
- Each extra class: £60

If PROJECT STEEL is registered in Classes 9, 41 and 42, renewal at today's fees would be:

**£245 + £60 + £60 = £365 every 10 years.**

Renewal can be made in the six months before expiry and up to six months late. Current late-renewal fee: £60.

Sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/renew-your-trade-mark
- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/trade-mark-forms-and-fees/trade-mark-forms-and-fees
