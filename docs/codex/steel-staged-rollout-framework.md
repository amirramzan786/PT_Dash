# Project Steel — Staged Rollout Framework

Status: Working product/launch rule
Branch context: `codex/founding20-beta-signups`

## Core principle
Steel should expand in controlled cohorts. Each stage exists to refine the product, measure real usage, fix friction, and prove stronger product-market fit before access widens.

Do not jump from the first test cohort directly to a full public launch.

## Stage 1 — Alpha
**Target cohort: 20 users**

Purpose:
- validate onboarding and account creation
- confirm core training, nutrition, progress and recovery flows work in real use
- identify blocking bugs and confusing UX
- measure whether people return voluntarily
- establish qualitative product value signals
- collect structured founder feedback

Founding offer:
- first 20 verified legitimate beta/alpha members receive core Steel Premium free for the lifetime of their account under the existing Founding 20 product rules
- this entitlement is permanent once legitimately allocated and is not conditional on later activity

Suggested duration:
- approximately 2–4 weeks of meaningful user activity, extended if major defects remain

Exit requirement:
- no critical blockers in core journeys
- onboarding and workout logging broadly reliable
- feedback system functioning
- enough active-user evidence to decide whether to iterate or expand

## Stage 2 — Beta
**Target cohort: 50 total users**

Purpose:
- validate Steel with a larger and more varied group
- test whether the product still feels intuitive without founder hand-holding
- compare warm vs cold acquisition
- compare UK vs international use
- test retention and repeated workout logging at a larger scale
- validate support and feedback operations
- refine positioning and the feature set

Rules:
- Founding 20 lifetime entitlement remains exclusive to the first 20 eligible verified founders
- users #21–50 do not automatically receive the lifetime Premium promise
- pricing does not need to be activated during this stage unless a separate commercial experiment is deliberately approved

Exit requirement:
- core product stable at 50 users
- recurring usage evident among a meaningful portion of the cohort
- no systemic onboarding, account, workout, nutrition or progress failures
- product feedback increasingly shifts from blockers to refinements

## Stage 3 — Extended Beta / Early Access
**Working target: 100 users**

Purpose:
- test broader acquisition channels
- validate behaviour outside the original founder network
- measure retention more reliably
- identify operational/support load
- test whether Steel's proposition is understandable without direct explanation
- begin stronger willingness-to-pay research

Potential experiments:
- landing-page proposition variants
- waitlist conversion
- pricing survey / willingness-to-pay conversations
- optional paid early-access experiment only after commercial gate approval

Exit requirement:
- stable core journeys
- acceptable support burden
- clear recurring-use segments
- evidence that users understand and value the unified training/nutrition/recovery/progress proposition

## Stage 4 — Controlled Early Access
**Working target: 250 users**

Purpose:
- prove the product can operate beyond a small hand-managed beta
- validate infrastructure, support and analytics under larger real-world use
- test repeatable acquisition
- test monetisation if prior commercial gates are passed

Focus:
- activation rate
- week-1 and week-4 retention
- workout completion/logging frequency
- nutrition/progress feature adoption
- support requests per active user
- conversion to paid where applicable

Do not expand further merely because the user cap has been reached. Expansion requires product and commercial evidence.

## Stage 5 — Pre-Launch / Release Candidate
**Working target: 500 users**

Purpose:
- final refinement before unrestricted/public launch
- validate scale, billing, entitlement handling, support, analytics and reliability
- remove remaining high-impact UX friction
- confirm a repeatable acquisition and monetisation path

This stage may be smaller or larger depending on data. User count is a ceiling/working target, not a reason to advance automatically.

## Stage 6 — Public Launch
No fixed user cap.

Public launch happens only when Steel is sufficiently refined and the commercial validation gate is credible.

Minimum strategic requirements before unrestricted launch:
1. exact paying customer is understood
2. painful problem/value proposition is validated
3. pricing/monetisation model has credible evidence
4. at least one realistic acquisition channel works
5. reasons users choose Steel over alternatives/doing nothing are understood
6. a concrete customer-acquisition plan exists
7. core app reliability and support processes are acceptable
8. analytics are capable of measuring activation, retention and conversion

## Advancement rule
Every stage ends with one of three decisions:

### GO
Expand to the next cohort because product stability, user value and usage evidence are strong enough.

### ITERATE
Hold the current cohort size while specific problems are fixed or tested again.

### STOP / REPOSITION
Do not keep scaling a weak proposition. Reassess the target customer, product proposition or commercial model before spending more build effort.

## Important interpretation
The cohort numbers are deliberate working gates:

**20 Alpha → 50 Beta → 100 Extended Beta → 250 Controlled Early Access → 500 Release Candidate → Public Launch**

They are not rigid vanity milestones. Steel advances when the evidence supports it, not simply because the previous cohort filled up.

## Relationship to Founding 20
The first 20 users remain a distinct founding cohort and should retain visible Founder recognition and their promised lifetime core Premium entitlement.

Later cohorts may receive different beta benefits, recognition or pricing experiments, but must not dilute or retrospectively change the Founding 20 promise.

## Parked future idea
Physical Founder merchandise may be considered later once Steel generates sufficient revenue. Do not build merchandise, fulfilment or reward infrastructure during Alpha/Beta unless separately approved.
