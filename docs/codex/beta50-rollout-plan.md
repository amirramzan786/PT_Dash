# Project Steel — Beta 50 rollout plan

**Branch:** `codex/founding20-beta-signups`
**Status:** Authoritative rollout plan for the cohort after Alpha 20.

## Purpose

Beta 50 exists to test whether Steel can move beyond a tightly managed founder alpha into a broader, less forgiving user base without losing reliability, clarity or value.

The first 20 remain the original Founding cohort. Beta 50 means **50 total active testers**, so the Beta expansion adds **30 new users**.

Do not move to Beta 50 until the Alpha 20 commercial gate produces a GO decision.

## What changes from Alpha to Beta

Alpha 20 is primarily for:
- core-product validation;
- bug discovery;
- onboarding friction;
- early retention;
- identifying the strongest value proposition;
- validating whether users genuinely care about Steel.

Beta 50 adds:
- broader user diversity;
- more independent/cold users;
- stronger competitor comparison;
- higher concurrency and backend usage;
- repeated onboarding at scale;
- more realistic support burden;
- early willingness-to-pay validation;
- measurement of whether Steel works beyond the founder circle.

## Cohort composition

Add **30 new Beta users** to the existing 20 Alpha founders.

Recommended new-user mix:
- 8 intermediate/advanced gym-goers;
- 6 beginners or returning trainees;
- 4 nutrition/macros-heavy users;
- 4 users who actively use a competing fitness app;
- 4 less-technical mainstream users;
- 4 wildcard users who match Steel's general fitness proposition but do not neatly fit the above categories.

Aim for approximately:
- 15 UK;
- 15 international.

English-speaking international markets should remain the priority until localisation is deliberately tested.

Avoid over-recruiting friends/family at this stage. The majority of the new 30 should be independent users who have no personal reason to be generous with feedback.

## Recruitment channels

Use a controlled mix of:
- Facebook fitness/community groups;
- Instagram posts/stories and small fitness creators;
- Snapchat network referrals where candidates genuinely fit;
- Reddit fitness communities where self-promotion rules allow;
- referrals from strong Alpha users;
- direct outreach to users of competitor fitness apps where appropriate.

Track source attribution for every Beta signup.

## Beta product offer

The original Founding 20 retain their locked lifetime core Steel Premium entitlement.

Users 21–50 **must not receive the Founding 20 lifetime entitlement** unless the product rules are deliberately changed before recruitment.

Recommended Beta 50 offer:

**Free Beta Access — no card required.**

Copy should make clear:
- access is free during Beta;
- Founding 20 lifetime places are already allocated;
- Beta participants still receive early access and influence product development;
- future public pricing is not yet locked.

Do not imply lifetime access for Beta 21–50.

## Beta 50 test objectives

### 1. Activation at larger scale
Measure:
- signup completion;
- email verification;
- onboarding completion;
- first plan/workout reached;
- first workout completed;
- nutrition/progress feature adoption.

### 2. Retention
Measure:
- Day 1 return;
- Day 7 return;
- Day 14 return;
- Day 30 return where cohort age allows;
- workouts completed per active user;
- proportion using more than one core Steel pillar.

### 3. Breadth of value
Steel should not only work for experienced lifters.

Compare activation and retention by:
- beginner vs experienced;
- UK vs international;
- warm/referral vs cold acquisition;
- competitor-app user vs non-app user;
- training-first vs nutrition-first user.

### 4. Competitor displacement signal
For users already using another fitness platform, capture:
- current app/tool;
- what they still prefer elsewhere;
- what Steel does better;
- whether Steel could replace part or all of their existing workflow;
- what would prevent switching.

Do not frame this as a forced head-to-head survey every session. Use targeted Beta checkpoints.

### 5. Support burden
Track:
- feedback items per user;
- actual support requests;
- repeated questions;
- recurring onboarding confusion;
- time required to resolve issues;
- issues that can be eliminated through product changes rather than support.

A product that needs constant founder intervention is not ready to scale.

### 6. Technical reliability
Track:
- failed signups;
- auth errors;
- failed data writes;
- workout-save failures;
- broken onboarding states;
- feedback submission failures;
- entitlement inconsistencies;
- frontend crashes/errors where safely measurable;
- material performance complaints.

Any high-severity data loss or account-access issue is a release blocker.

## Pricing validation begins here

Beta 50 is the first stage where Steel should begin **structured pricing research**, but no user should be charged solely because Beta 50 has started.

### Alpha pricing signal
Alpha users may already have answered hypothetical willingness-to-pay questions. Treat those answers as directional only.

### Beta pricing research
At suitable checkpoints, ask non-leading questions such as:

**If Steel became a paid product after Beta, which best describes you?**
- I would probably pay for it.
- I might pay depending on the price.
- I would only use it if free.
- I am not sure yet.

Then, where appropriate:

**At what monthly price would Steel start to feel expensive for you?**

and/or test discrete price concepts without presenting them as final pricing.

Working price concepts can include the previously discussed range, but **do not hard-code a public price until evidence supports it**.

Do not ask users to validate price before they have experienced enough of the product to judge its value.

## Paid conversion test — later within Beta only if justified

A real paid test is stronger evidence than hypothetical intent, but it should only be introduced when:
- Alpha passed;
- Beta users are retaining;
- major UX/reliability issues are under control;
- a meaningful number of independent users report willingness to pay.

If those conditions are met, design a separate controlled pricing experiment before charging anyone.

Do not enable billing automatically as part of this rollout plan.

## Feedback programme

Continue the in-app Beta Feedback experience.

Use **You asked. We listened.** to visibly close the feedback loop.

Release notes should show meaningful changes without flooding users with trivial internal updates.

Beta feedback should continue to be classified as:
- bug;
- friction;
- feature request;
- value signal.

Also add a priority dimension:
- blocker;
- high;
- medium;
- low.

## Beta 50 success signals

The exact progression gate should be reviewed against real Alpha baseline data, but Beta 50 should broadly demonstrate:
- reliable onboarding and account creation;
- healthy first-workout completion;
- meaningful Day 7 and Day 14 retention;
- repeated real-world workout use;
- clear evidence that at least one Steel value proposition resonates strongly;
- independent/cold users performing reasonably close to warm users;
- no unresolved critical data/auth/reliability defects;
- support burden that remains manageable;
- growing willingness-to-pay evidence;
- at least some users saying Steel could replace an existing fitness tracking workflow.

## Decision after Beta 50

Possible outcomes:

### GO — move to Extended Beta 100
Proceed when product reliability, user value and retention are all materially strong enough to justify another controlled expansion.

### ITERATE — remain at 50
Stay at Beta 50 when there is evidence of value but one or more material problems remain. Fix, publish via **You asked. We listened.**, and re-measure before recruiting more users.

### STOP / REPOSITION
Do not scale the existing proposition when independent users repeatedly fail to activate/return, value signals are weak, and fixes do not materially improve outcomes. Diagnose whether Steel needs a narrower positioning, significant product change or commercial rethink before further expansion.

## Guardrails

- User count is not the objective; product evidence is.
- Never recruit extra users simply to hide weak metrics.
- Founding 20 lifetime entitlement remains permanent once validly allocated.
- Beta users 21–50 do not inherit lifetime entitlement.
- Do not introduce billing merely because Beta 50 begins.
- Do not add major new features purely because individual users request them.
- Prioritise repeated friction and repeated value signals over one-off opinions.
- Preserve privacy and collect only analytics needed to make product decisions.

## Exit from Beta 50

Before moving to 100 users, produce a short Beta 50 review containing:
1. cohort composition;
2. activation funnel;
3. retention;
4. feature usage;
5. feedback themes;
6. reliability/support issues;
7. UK vs international comparison;
8. cold vs warm/referral comparison;
9. competitor displacement evidence;
10. pricing/willingness-to-pay evidence;
11. changes shipped through **You asked. We listened.**;
12. explicit GO / ITERATE / STOP decision.

The next cohort is not invited until that decision is recorded.
