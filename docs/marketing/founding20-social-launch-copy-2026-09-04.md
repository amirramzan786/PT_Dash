## Visual direction

- Near-black Steel background (`#070b11` family).
- Aged bronze/gold highlights matching the Steel identity.
- Spartan helmet imagery should feel monumental and premium, not cosplay.
- Keep the composition sparse and cinematic.
- Make `FOUNDING 20` the visual anchor.
- `£0 FOR LIFE` should be immediately legible but not look like a discount-store promotion.
- Use subtle smoke, stone/metal texture and bronze rim lighting.
- Do not use fake app ratings, fake testimonials or fake signup counts.
- Mobile-first readability.
